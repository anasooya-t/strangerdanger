Stranger: u there?

It was 11:30 am when Adeen Asad Mirza opened her iPad and decided to talk to online strangers from her bedroom in Dubai. Mirza, who is now an 18-year-old woman, used to speak with anonymous strangers online since she was 10.

“Tons of weird experiences. I can write you an essay on the shit I have seen.”

Omegle.com was the first few websites that popularised the idea of talking to online strangers. Developed by Leif K. Brooks, the website used to get upto 66 million monthly visits before it shut down in 2023 due to numerous lawsuits involving groomed minors.

“In some ways, what we feared has happened - a Hydra effect, where closing a popular webcam platform like Omegle leads to the problem becoming more diffuse,” said researcher Paul Bleakley.
