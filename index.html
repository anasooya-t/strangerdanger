I Want to Talk to Strangers

It was 11:30 am when Adeen Asad Mirza opened her iPad and decided to talk to online strangers from her bedroom in Dubai. Mirza who is now an 18-year-old woman preparing to go to college used to speak with anonymous strangers online with her friends since she was 10-years-old through websites like umingle.com...

“Tons of weird experiences. I can write you an essay on the shit I have seen,” said Mirza as she talked with me on the website.

Often known by different names these roulette-styled chatting platforms allowed users to speak with strangers of any age without an account. Mirza had first found out about such websites from her friends from school who were already using them even before they had their own phones.

The number of random video chatting websites on the internet like umingle.com that provides services where children can openly connect and speak with adult strangers without any oversight or regulation has increased.

Omegle.com was the first few websites that popularised the idea of talking to online strangers. Developed by Leif K. Brooks an 18-years-old software programmer in Oregon, the website used to get upto 66 million monthly visits...
